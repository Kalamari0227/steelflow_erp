## A — 미니어처 제철소

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a beautiful high-fidelity desktop web application design mockup for SteelFlow 3D, a fictional steelworks ERP visualized as a miniature industrial simulation game. Physical plant topology: coastline and water to the right; raw-material ship unloading and raw stockpiles at upper right; conveyors to raw preparation and blast furnace upper left; a short dedicated hot-metal railway to converter/refining, then continuous casting and slab/bloom/billet yard; elongated rolling sheds in the middle; cold rolling and coating branches below; finished coil warehouse next to truck loading, external freight rail yard and a separate finished-product quay lower right. Use irregular purpose-built industrial parcels, long realistic sheds, round tanks, stackers, gantry cranes, pipe racks and rail sidings; NEVER a checkerboard of identical buildings, NEVER a diagram of boxes. Provide a connected service road network with broad bends, lane markings, gate, vehicle staging and turning aprons; tracks must not run through unrelated buildings; raw bulk conveyors, road haulage, hot-metal tracks and dispatch rail must be visually distinct. Trucks, slab carriers, forklifts, locomotives and ship cranes should read as actively working in a frozen moment of an animated simulation. Vehicles stay on sensible lanes / loading bays. No random roads or decorative crisscross arrows. One selected synthetic order SO-1024 and material SL-024-A are linked visually, amber selection for the material and subdued teal route segments. Show one current material location; do not duplicate the same material in several places as if simultaneously present. The UI implies orbit, zoom, directional pan, play/pause, 1x 2x 4x and follow material. Small top brand SteelFlow, small text SYNTHETIC DEMO. Few concise Korean labels are fine but avoid dense text. No real company logo, no watermark, no science fiction, no dark cyberpunk, no photograph of an actual factory.
