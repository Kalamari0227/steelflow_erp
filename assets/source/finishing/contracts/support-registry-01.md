## Static frame versus rotating material surface

Anvils are physically stationary. Their socket node and every ancestor must have no operational or follower motion. Checking only primary motion_function_ids is insufficient if an ancestor is a mechanical follower. The fixed anvil is not the moving blade or clamp jaw.

Some looper fixed-roll barrels are rotational followers: 'fixed' means a fixed center, not a nonrotating mesh. Thus the earlier idea of attaching a chain=[] socket to that rotating node is invalid. The approved looper socket is instead a root-frame stationary geometric tangent witness at the actual top of that fixed-center closed cylinder, normal+Y; its real contact surface is the named barrel. This is the existing static tangent-contact pattern, not an extra lift or a claim that the cylinder is stationary. Prove the same point against actual rotating decoded triangles across the declared scalar range, including mesh segmentation's worst angular phase. The finite5 producer samples cannot by themselves certify every angle. Reject a moving-center/lifted barrel, wrong radius/height, open/incomplete surface or tangent point no longer contacting it. Existing feed/looper channels retain their exact bound ranges and actual motion; no support follows a channel secretly.

The finite4m connected strip path must separately preserve its actual length/volume, support tangency and clearance through lift/release, pause, endpoint and handoff, using the existing looper functions. A rest socket within1cm does not certify the full stock path, contact normal, outgoing/incoming custody or full-machine collision. Ordinary public COM/contact and same-ID boundary semantics remain unchanged. Welder/cutter N:M uses actual T10 terminal lineage plus separate mesh boundary proof per weld-cut-fixtures.md, not renamed T58 materials.
